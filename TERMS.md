## 1. What Saturis is

Saturis is a calculator and a logbook for swimming pool and spa water chemistry. It
takes water test readings that you supply and computes quantities of pool chemicals
based on the pool volume and product strengths you tell it about.

## 2. What Saturis is not

**Saturis is not professional advice.** It does not inspect your pool, verify your
readings, or know anything about your water that you have not typed into it.

Its output is only as good as three things you control: the accuracy of your test kit,
the accuracy of your pool volume, and whether the product in your hand is the one you
selected in the app. A wrong volume or a mis-read colour chart produces a wrong dose,
and the app cannot detect either.

**Product labels take precedence over Saturis, always.** Where a manufacturer's
instructions differ from what the app calculates, follow the manufacturer.

## 3. Chemical safety — your responsibility

Pool chemicals are hazardous. Concentrated acid causes burns. Mixing chlorine products
with acid releases chlorine gas. Mixing calcium hypochlorite with trichlor can start a
fire. By using Saturis you accept that handling and adding chemicals is entirely your
responsibility, and you agree to:

- read and follow the label and safety data sheet for every product you use;
- add chemicals to water and never water to chemicals;
- never mix two pool chemicals together, in any container, feeder or skimmer;
- allow the time between different chemicals that the app specifies;
- wear appropriate gloves and eye protection;
- store chemicals separately, dry, and out of reach of children and animals;
- keep everyone out of the water until sanitiser levels are back within a safe range;
- engage a qualified pool technician when something is beyond your competence.

If you are unsure about anything, do not guess and do not rely on the app. Get
professional help.

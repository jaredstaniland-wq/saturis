# Privacy policy — Saturis

**Last updated: 30 July 2026**
**Applies to: Saturis for Android, version 1.0.0 and later**

## The short version

Saturis does not collect anything. There is no account, no server, no analytics, and no
advertising. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your own phone.

The app does not request internet access, so it cannot transmit your information
anywhere even by accident.

## Who we are

Saturis is published by Saturis Software. For anything in this policy, contact
**saturisapp@gmail.com**.

## What information the app handles

Everything below is stored only on your device, in the app's own private storage area
that other apps cannot read.

| What | Why the app has it |
| --- | --- |
| Pool details — name, volume, surface type, sanitiser type, region and units | To calculate doses that are correct for your pool |
| Water test readings — chlorine, pH, alkalinity, calcium hardness, cyanuric acid, salt, borate, temperature | To calculate doses and show your history |
| Notes you type against a test | Because you chose to write them |
| Chemical products you select, and any prices you enter | To express doses in the units of the product you own, and to show what a dose costs |
| Maintenance tasks and reminder times | To schedule reminders on your device |
| App preferences — units, region, theme | To keep the app set up the way you left it |

## What we do not do

- We do not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any of it.
- We do not have a server that holds your data, because there is no server.
- We do not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising SDKs of any kind.
- We do not ask you to create an account, and we never ask for your email address.
- We do not track your location. The app requests no location permission.
- We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
- We do not build advertising profiles or share anything with data brokers.

## Permissions the app requests, and why

| Permission | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Notifications | To show the maintenance reminders you set up. Requested only at the moment you switch reminders on, and the app works fully without it. |
| Run at startup | To re-register your reminders after the phone restarts, so a schedule you set does not silently stop working. |

Saturis does not request internet access.

## Backups

Android's built-in backup service may include Saturis' data file in the backup your
phone already makes to your Google account, which is how your pool history follows you
to a new device. That backup is between you, your device and Google — it is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and Saturis Software has
no access to it.

You can turn device backup off in your Android system settings at any time.

## Purchases

The one-time Saturis Pro unlock is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment
details. Google tells the app only whether this Google account owns the unlock. Payment
handling is covered by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
and the Google Play Terms of Service.

## Exports and sharing

If you choose to export a backup file or a CSV of your log, Saturis writes that file and
hands it to Android's share sheet. Where it goes from there is entirely your choice, and
the app is not involved once you have picked a destination.

## Children

Saturis is not directed at children. It gives instructions for handling pool chemicals
and is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any
age, because we do not collect information at all.

## Your rights

Because your data never leaves your device, there is nothing for us to hand over,
correct or delete on your behalf. You are in full control:

- **See it** — Settings › Export a backup produces a readable file of everything the app holds.
- **Move it** — the same file can be restored on another device.
- **Delete it** — Settings › Erase everything removes all pools, tests, tasks and preferences from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes them.

If you are in the EU or UK, the GDPR rights of access, rectification, erasure,
restriction, portability and objection all apply — and all of them are exercised
directly in the app, because we are not a data controller for anything you enter. If
you are in California, the CCPA and CPRA rights apply on the same basis; we have never
sold or shared personal information and have no plans to.

## Security

Your data sits in the app's private storage, protected by Android's application sandbox
and by whatever device encryption your phone uses — on any modern Android device that
means the data is encrypted at rest by the operating system. Keeping a screen lock on
your phone is the single most effective thing you can do to protect it.

## Changes to this policy

If Saturis ever gains a feature that handles information differently, this policy will
be updated before that feature ships, and the change will be described in the app's
release notes. The current version always lives at https://jaredstaniland-wq.github.io/saturis/privacy.html.

If a future version of Saturis were ever to collect or transmit data, that would be
opt-in, explained clearly in the app, and disclosed here first.
